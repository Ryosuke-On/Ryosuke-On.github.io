Software Design and Analysis Laboratory

I study how much test quality matters when LLM agents fix bugs on their own. With Defects4J and RepairAgent, I am testing whether coverage and mutation score can predict how accurately the agent repairs a bug.

My other theme is the pause (ma) and speech style in conversations with AI. I am building FrAIend Memory, a journaling app that talks in Kansai dialect and doesn’t mark your messages as read right away.

As an undergraduate, I used BERT to predict the results of the NuSMV model checker (whether a property holds, and the counterexample if not). The idea is to sidestep the cost of state explosion with approximate prediction, so that model checking becomes easier to adopt.
